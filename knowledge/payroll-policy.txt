PAYFLOW PAYROLL POLICY

1. Payroll is generated for each employee on a monthly basis.

2. Earned salary is calculated using the employee's applicable base salary and the number of payable attendance days in the payroll month.

3. A PRESENT attendance day counts as 1 payable day.

4. A HALF_DAY attendance counts as 0.5 payable day.

5. ABSENT and LEAVE attendance do not count as payable days.

6. Performance bonuses are added to the employee's earned salary.

7. Overtime compensation is calculated using the approved overtime hours and the applicable overtime rate.

8. Pending advances applicable to the payroll month are deducted from the employee's payroll.

9. Other deductions are subtracted from the employee's gross payable amount when applicable.

10. Net salary is calculated as:

Net Salary = Earned Salary + Bonus + Overtime - Advance Deduction - Other Deductions

11. A payroll record initially has DRAFT status.

12. A payroll can be finalized after payroll generation.

13. Once payroll is finalized, the payroll record becomes FINALIZED.

14. Finalized payroll records must not be modified through normal payroll operations.

15. Payroll calculations are performed by the PayFlow backend. AI features may explain payroll calculations but must not independently calculate or modify payroll records.
