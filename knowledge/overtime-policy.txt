PAYFLOW OVERTIME POLICY

1. Overtime compensation is provided for approved overtime work performed by an employee.

2. Overtime is recorded for a specific employee and working date.

3. Overtime hours must be greater than zero.

4. Overtime records cannot be created for future dates.

5. Overtime records cannot be created for inactive employees.

6. The overtime amount is calculated by the PayFlow backend using:

Overtime Amount = Overtime Hours × Overtime Rate

7. The overtime rate is determined when the overtime record is created.

8. Overtime compensation for a payroll month is included in that employee's payroll calculation.

9. AI features may explain overtime calculations and applicable policy information but must not independently modify overtime records or payroll.
