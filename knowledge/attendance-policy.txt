PAYFLOW ATTENDANCE POLICY

1. Attendance is recorded for each employee for a specific working date.

2. Each employee can have only one attendance record for a given date.

3. The supported attendance statuses are:
   PRESENT
   ABSENT
   HALF_DAY
   LEAVE

4. PRESENT attendance contributes 1 payable day.

5. HALF_DAY attendance contributes 0.5 payable day.

6. ABSENT attendance contributes 0 payable days.

7. LEAVE attendance contributes 0 payable days.

8. Attendance records cannot be created for future dates.

9. Attendance records cannot be created for inactive employees.

10. Attendance records associated with a finalized payroll month are locked and cannot be modified through normal attendance operations.

11. Attendance information is used by the payroll system to calculate the employee's earned salary.

12. AI features may explain attendance and payroll rules but must not modify attendance records.
