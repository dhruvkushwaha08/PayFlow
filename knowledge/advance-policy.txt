PAYFLOW ADVANCE POLICY

1. An employee may receive a salary advance recorded in the PayFlow system.

2. Every advance is associated with a specific employee.

3. Advance amounts must be greater than zero.

4. Advances cannot be created for future dates.

5. Advances cannot be created for inactive employees.

6. A newly created advance has PENDING status.

7. An advance may transition from PENDING to DEDUCTED when it is applied to payroll.

8. A PENDING advance may be cancelled when it is no longer applicable.

9. An advance that has been DEDUCTED or CANCELLED must not be deleted through normal operations.

10. Pending advances applicable up to the payroll month-end are deducted when the corresponding payroll is finalized.

11. Advance deductions reduce the employee's net salary.

12. AI features may explain advance deductions and policy rules but must not independently modify advance records or payroll.
