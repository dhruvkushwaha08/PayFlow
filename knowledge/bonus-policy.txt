PAYFLOW BONUS POLICY

1. Bonuses may be provided to employees as additional compensation.

2. A bonus is associated with a specific employee and bonus date.

3. Supported bonus types are:
   PERCENTAGE
   FIXED

4. For a FIXED bonus, the specified bonus amount is added to the employee's payroll.

5. For a PERCENTAGE bonus, the bonus percentage is applied to the employee's earned salary for the applicable payroll month.

6. A percentage bonus cannot exceed 100 percent.

7. Bonuses cannot be created for future dates.

8. Bonuses cannot be created for inactive employees.

9. Bonus calculations are performed by the PayFlow backend during payroll generation.

10. Bonus amounts are included in the employee's payroll calculation.

11. AI features may explain bonus calculations and policy information but must not independently modify bonuses or payroll records.
